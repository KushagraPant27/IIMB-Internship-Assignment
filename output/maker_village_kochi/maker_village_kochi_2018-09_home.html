Electronics Incubator


The Electronics Incubator is a joint initiative between, Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology(MeitY) and Indian Institute of Information Technology and Management Kerala (IIITM K), the nodal agency of the project, Kerala Startup Mission and Govt of Kerala to promote consumer electronics based innovation and set up labs and innovation centers that focus on the current trend and emerging technologies of disruptive nature to bring India to the forefront of innovation in the electronics space.

Read More
Electronics Workbench

The incubatees will be provided soldering stations, Multimeters , function generators, DSO etc for the development of their product and all types of tinkering.

Testing and Measuring Equipments, workstations, 3D printers

The incubator will house advanced equipments for measurement and testing like Mixed Domain Oscilloscope, LCR meter, Bench top Multimeter etc , high-end workstations for product design and 3D printers for prototype development

PCB Assembly line

A 25000 CPH assembly line for SMT boards will be developed with the aim of assembly of PCB so that startups can develop their final products of the highest quality

DIY innovation centre

The centre will have all major opensource boards like Arduino, Raspberry pi, Beagle Bone , Intel edison, IOT boards , actuators, robotic kits , sensors etc that would enable rapid protoyping for the startups

Annexe

An annexe is being inaugurated at IIITMK’s IoT lab inside the IIITMK Campus, Technopark for the benefit of local startups who can be incubated in the Trivandrum Region.

Incubation

A 25000 sq ft facility has been developed at the Technology Innovation Zone, KINFRA Hitech Park, Kochi, by Kerala Startup Mission for the Electronics Incubator.

Pre-incubation Stage
1-3 months

Pre incubation stage mainly focuses on ideation stage, where technologist having a potential innovative idea can be provided with a co working space and limited access to equipments and allied facilities.

Incubation stage
6-12 months

Companies start product development to build a market viable prototype and prepare themselves for marketing. The Electronics Incubator at this stage will allot them specific seats/modules along with funds in the range of 1 to 2 Lakhs INR for promising incubatees with right mentorship.

Accelerator stage and graduation
3-6 months

Accelerator stage will be a business tools box and a holistic business advisory service, for the startups. . The startups shall be presented to VC’s and investors and the electronics incubator arrange an investment in ten companies in a year upto a maximum of 20 Lakhs INR depending on market viability and innovativeness of the product
