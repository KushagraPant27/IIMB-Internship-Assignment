Lab32 is a six month long comprehensive incubation program, tailor-made for early-stage tech product startups. T-Hub presents FinTech Arcade
Currently taking applications
A market access program for Fintech startups seeking to scaleup by exploring new strategic partnerships. It is imperative for startups to find right corporate partners who could license, buy-out their products or on-board them as vendors.
Fintech Arcade comes in to open up multitude of opportunities to expand the market. It will bring together corporates looking to speed date with startups in fintech space and explore possible partnerships.
