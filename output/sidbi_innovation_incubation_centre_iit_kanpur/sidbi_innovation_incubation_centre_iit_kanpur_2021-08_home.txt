About Us
SIIC, IIT Kanpur came into existence when in 2000 SIDBI approached IIT Kanpur to form an Incubation center. The idea was to deepen the entrepreneurship and incubation culture of the institute.
SIIC, IIT Kanpur aims to develop cutting-edge technologies grounded in science and engineering Innovations to solve pressing problems of the country, focusing on the underprivileged strata. It provides the right space and opportunities for converting your startup ideas into products and business.
We are an organization of innovators, researchers, startup enthusiasts, and aspiring entrepreneurs. Our open and non-hierarchical work culture makes your journey from the lab to the market an easy one. With your talent and our technology, we build strategies for your startups and help you execute them in relevant markets.
SIIC provides young entrepreneurs and investors with mentoring (professor/ alumni/ Industry veterans), legal and IPR assistance, access to labs, proper infrastructure to facilitate tech transfer and connect to relevant venture funding avenues.
