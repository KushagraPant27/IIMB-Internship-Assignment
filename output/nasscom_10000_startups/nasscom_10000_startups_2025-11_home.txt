Veltech–Nasscom Partnership to Drive Digital Skilling
In a significant step towards bridging the industry-academia skill gap, IT-ITeS Sector Skills Council (SSC) Nasscom has signed an MoU with Veltech University to integrate two FutureSkills Prime courses as mandatory certifications for 2nd and 3rd-year students pursuing Information Technology and Computer Science .
This strategic collaboration aims to ensure that students are equipped with industry-relevant digital competencies , empowering them to stay ahead in an evolving tech landscape.
Under this partnership, the university will embed FutureSkills Prime learning modules directly into its curriculum, fostering a culture of continuous learning and innovation among students.
Goal: The initiative targets the completion of 3,500+ certifications by February 2026 , marking a strong commitment to nurturing a digitally proficient and future-ready talent pool.
By aligning academic learning with industry standards, IT-ITeS SSC Nasscom and Veltech University are together shaping the next generation of tech professionals who are prepared to thrive in India’s digital economy.
