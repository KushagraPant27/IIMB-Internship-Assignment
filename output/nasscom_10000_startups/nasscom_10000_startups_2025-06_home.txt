SSC Nasscom Joins Forces with Bihar Government to Drive Skill Development at “Skills Se Samriddhi Tak”
SSC Nasscom participated in the “Skills Se Samriddhi Tak” event, organized by the Bihar Institute of Public Administration & Rural Development (BIPARD) and the Bihar Skill Development Mission, Government of Bihar. The event aimed to transform Bihar into a skilled and prosperous state by 2047.
The gathering saw the presence of esteemed dignitaries, including policymakers, industry leaders, awarding bodies, academic institutions, and vocational training organizations. Together, they showcased ongoing efforts in building a robust skill development ecosystem across India, with a special focus on Bihar’s initiatives and progress.
