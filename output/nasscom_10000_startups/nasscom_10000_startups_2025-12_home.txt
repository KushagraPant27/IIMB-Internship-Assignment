Highlight of the week from Nasscom
Spotlight
Nasscom-DSCI Annual Information Security Summit (AISS) 2025
AISS is an industry-led conference that brings together a powerful mix of stakeholders, cutting-edge themes, and immersive experiences. Across three days, the summit unfolds through multiple tracks, diverse session formats, and interactive engagements that create a rich learning and networking environment for the cybersecurity community.
This year’s conversations will dive deep into some of the most pressing and transformative areas shaping the future of security. Expect thought-provoking discussions on security technology leadership, geopolitics and cyber diplomacy, global market opportunities for Indian cybersecurity capabilities, privacy-enhancing technologies, and the evolving security landscape at the intersection of AI and quantum. The summit will also explore the next wave of cybercrimes and the tools needed to stay ahead of emerging threats.
AISS will also feature the launch of key research reports, including the Indian Cyber Security Product Landscape by DSCI, the State of AI Adoption for Cyber Security in India created with Palo Alto Networks, and Safeguarding India’s Digital Health , developed jointly with HIMSS to define a framework for secure healthcare infrastructure.
To enable deeper sector-specific conversations, the summit will host multiple focused sub-summits and forums spanning quantum security, healthcare, digital forensics, operational technology, privacy and policy, as well as leadership and education. Dedicated platforms such as PULSE and the Women in Security Forum will offer further opportunities for skill-building, dialogue, and community growth.
With over 350 global leaders , 3,500+ delegates , 11 masterclasses and workshops , 15+ sub-events , and sessions hosted across two locations , AISS 2025 promises unmatched access to insights, expertise, and collaboration.
