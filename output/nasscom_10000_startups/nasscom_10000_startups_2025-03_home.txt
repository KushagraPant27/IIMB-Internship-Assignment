Karnataka Govt. & Nasscom Join Forces to Strengthen IP Ecosystem in IT & ITeS
Karnataka Innovation and Technology Society (KITS), under the Department of Electronics, IT, BT, and S&T, Government of Karnataka, has signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with Nasscom to drive IP awareness, protection, and commercialization in Karnataka’s IT and ITeS sector. The MoU was formalized in the presence of Shri Priyank Kharge, Honourable Minister for Information Technology, Biotechnology, and Rural Development & Panchayati Raj, Dr. Ekroop Caur, Secretary, ITBT, Daljeet Kumar, Deputy Secretary, ITBT, Government of Karnataka, and Mr. Rajesh Nambiar, President, Nasscom. This strategic collaboration aims to position Karnataka as a premier innovation hub by fostering IP creation, strengthening technology development, and accelerating commercialization efforts, ultimately driving economic growth. During the event, Honourable Minister Shri Priyank Kharge engaged with industry leaders on the need to cultivate a strong innovation culture and significantly boost IP generation from Karnataka over the next five years.
