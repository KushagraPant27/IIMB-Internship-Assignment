Union Budget 2025: Focus on Driving Growth Through Innovation
Nasscom welcomes the government's commitment to innovation and ease of doing business, reinforcing India’s global leadership ambitions. The proposed DeepTech Fund aligns with industry recommendations and will help scale startups into global champions. Additionally, Nasscom has been working with the government on simplifying international taxation, with progress seen in transfer pricing assessments and Safe Harbour Rules. The organization will continue collaborating with policymakers to implement these measures and analyze further budget details.
