    Spotlight

    Highlight of the week from Nasscom
    Empowering Women Leaders to Architect the Future of Innovation and Impact!

    The first Nasscom Women Product Champions (NWPC) Roadshow in Mumbai brought together 80+ participants from 49 organizations, igniting a movement of collaboration, innovation, and impact—scaling ideas, breaking barriers, and empowering women leaders to architect a world driven by purpose and precision: a blueprint for building the future. From reimagining product design for global impact to driving innovation that inspires trust, every conversation underscored the power of bold thinking and community-driven leadership. Speakers shared actionable insights on scaling products with precision, bridging the gap between creativity and security, and embracing technology to shape industries. This is NWPC in action—fuelling the future, one impactful idea at a time.
