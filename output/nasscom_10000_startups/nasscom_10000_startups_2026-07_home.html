    India’s Next Energy Revolution

    #Sustainability

    India’s AI-driven energy demand is accelerating green hydrogen adoption, targeting 5 million tonnes by 2030 for energy security.

    KEY TAKEAWAYS
    • AI-driven computing and data centers are reshaping electricity demand, creating unprecedented pressure on energy infrastructure and supply.
    • Intermittent solar and wind power make large-scale energy storage essential for grid stability and efficient utilization.
    • Green hydrogen offers India a path to decarbonization, energy independence, and replacing most gray hydrogen by 2030.

    Charting the Future: AI and Global Power

    #AIregulation

    As AI reshapes geopolitics and great-power rivalry, global cooperation is crucial to ensure responsible development and governance.

    KEY TAKEAWAYS
    • AI is rapidly reshaping geopolitics, amplifying great power rivalries and creating new risks to global stability.
    • Intensifying competition among nations makes collaborative AI governance difficult, increasing fears of dangerous superintelligence development.
    • Global leaders and businesses must urgently coordinate AI regulation to prevent catastrophic misuse and weaponization.

    Leading Through People: A BPM Leadership Story

    #FutureOfWork

    Kalpak Huddar of Foundever highlights the BPM industry as a dynamic career path, emphasizing upskilling, growth, leadership, and future-ready talent.

    KEY TAKEAWAYS
    • India’s CX industry has evolved from cost-driven outsourcing into a people-centric ecosystem delivering value, innovation, and business transformation.
    • Continuous upskilling and rapid internal mobility make the CX sector a powerful engine for leadership development.
    • Beyond business impact, the CX industry drives inclusion, employability, and meaningful social progress through people-first growth.

    The Future of CX: An AI Story

    #AIForCX

    EXL’s Smart Agent Assist uses Gen AI to boost compliance, resolutions, and CX across 20M-plus customer service interactions globally.

    KEY TAKEAWAYS
    • EXL’s Smart Agent Assist uses generative AI to deliver real-time guidance, improving compliance and first-call resolution.
    • Speech-to-text, automated summaries, and AI-driven audits reduce call times while enhancing customer experience efficiency.
    • Handling over 20 million calls, EXL plans to scale Smart Agent Assist beyond 50 million.
