Makers Honor Compendium Launched at NTLF 2026
Rooted in the belief that India’s next era of leadership will be shaped not merely by scale, but by quality of thought and clarity of purpose, the Makers Honor Compendium was launched at NTLF 2026. It brings together the journeys of our Makers Honor winners along with other outstanding applicants, documenting the curiosity, resilience, and responsible innovation that define modern Indian engineering.
More than a collection of achievements, this compendium captures the human stories behind technological progress. Each journey reflects persistence, experimentation, learning, and renewal, reminding us that innovation is rarely linear; it is iterative and deeply human. Together, these narratives form a living archive of how engineering excellence in India is practiced and advanced, standing as a testament to the minds and values behind every breakthrough.
