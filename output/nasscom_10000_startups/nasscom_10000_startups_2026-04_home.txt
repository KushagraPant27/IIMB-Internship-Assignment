Nasscom AI-Assisted Developer Workshop
The Nasscom AI-Assisted Developer Workshop serves as the foundational phase of a structured learning journey designed to equip developers with AI-assisted development capabilities. As Phase 1 of the Explore–Engineer–Excel roadmap, it introduces key concepts and practical approaches, progressively building towards more advanced, real-world applications.
Running in parallel is the Agentic AI Hackathon , centred on industry use cases, enabling participants to apply their learning through hands-on, solution-driven challenges.
Participants may choose to register for the Developer Workshop , the Hackathon , or both, based on their learning objectives. Registrations for subsequent phases will be announced in due course.
