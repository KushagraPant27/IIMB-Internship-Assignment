About Us Overview

Overview

Nasscom, a not-for-profit industry association, is the apex body for the $245 billion technology industry in India, an industry that has made a phenomenal contribution to India's GDP, exports, employment, infrastructure and global visibility. In India, this industry provides the highest employment in the private sector.

Established in 1988 and ever since, nasscom’s relentless pursuit has been to constantly support the technology industry, in the latter’s continued journey towards seeking trust and respect from varied stakeholders, even as it reorients itself time and again to remain innovative,without ever losing its humane and friendly touch.

Nasscom is focused on building the architecture integral to the development of the technology sector through policy advocacy, and help in setting up the strategic direction for the sector to unleash its potential and dominate newer frontiers.

Nasscom’s members, 3000+, constitute 90% of the industry’s revenue and have enabled the association to spearhead initiatives at local, national and global levels. In turn, the technology industry has gained recognition as a global powerhouse.

Our Vision

When the world thinks, Digital, the world will think India

Our Mission

To help the technology products and services industry in India to be trustworthy and innovative across the globe.

Our Strategic Imperative

Our Strategic Imperative includes building the tech ecosystem and industry narrative with focus on:

  • Talent

    Reskill and Upskill India’s workforce to ensure TALENT is Future Ready in terms of New Age Skills and Jobs.

  • Innovation

    Strengthen India's Innovation Quotient and Impact.

  • Markets

    Create new Market Access in India and across the Globe.

  • Trust

    Drive policy advocacy to advance Trust, Innovation and Ease of Doing Business.

  • Inclusion

    Champion Equal Opportunity and Diversity in everything we do.
