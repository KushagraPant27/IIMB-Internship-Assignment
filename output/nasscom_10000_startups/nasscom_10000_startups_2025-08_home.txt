India–UK FTA: A Landmark Move for Cross-Border Tech Talent Mobility
Nasscom extends its gratitude to Hon’ble Prime Minister Shri Narendra Modi, Minister of Commerce and Industry Shri Piyush Goyal, and the Government of India for championing a key industry priority in the India–UK Free Trade Agreement (FTA).
The inclusion of the Double Contributions Convention (DCC) —a first in any trade agreement—is a landmark achievement that will enable Indian professionals deputed to the UK to retain their Indian social security benefits. This progressive step not only facilitates cross-border mobility but also mitigates the burden of dual social security contributions, aligning trade policy with the evolving needs of a globally mobile technology workforce.
Nasscom remains committed to working alongside the government and industry to co-create progressive, future-ready frameworks that reinforce India’s position as a global technology leader.
