Nasscom Announces Srikanth Velamakanni as Chair, Kishor Patil as Vice Chair
Nasscom today announced the appointment of Srikanth Velamakanni, Co-Founder and Group Chief Executive of Fractal, as its Chairperson. He succeeds Sindhu Gangadharan, Managing Director, SAP Labs India, who completes a distinguished term as Chairperson in April 2026. Alongside this, the Nasscom Executive Council has also elected Kishor Patil, Co-Founder, CEO & Managing Director at KPIT as the new Vice Chairperson.
Srikanth assumes the role after serving as Vice Chairperson of Nasscom and as an Executive Council member for over six years. As co-founder of one of India's earliest and most prominent AI-first enterprises, he brings deep expertise in enterprise AI, analytics, responsible technology, and global go-to-market to the role, at a defining moment for the industry.
Working closely with Rajesh Nambiar, President, Nasscom, and the Executive Council, Srikanth will steer the industry's agenda across a set of interconnected priorities that reflect where the global technology landscape is headed.
