Future-Ready Digital Skills Reach Tier-3 Classrooms
An MoU has been signed between IT-ITeS SSC Nasscom and Akal Academy , marking an important milestone in making future-ready skills accessible to students in Tier-3 cities . Through this partnership, more than 3,000 students will benefit from direct access to digital skills in emerging technologies , integrated seamlessly into their academic journey.
As part of this collaboration, 15 industry-relevant courses will be built directly into the curriculum, ensuring that students don’t just study theory but also develop practical, job-ready expertise. This holistic approach will prepare every learner for thriving careers in domains such as Artificial Intelligence, Cloud Computing, Cybersecurity, Data Science, and other high-demand technologies .
How this MoU will help:
Promoting Digital Inclusion in Tier-3 Cities – Students from smaller towns will gain the same exposure to cutting-edge technologies as those in metros, reducing the digital divide.
Curriculum Integration for Holistic Learning – Embedding 15 Nasscom-approved courses into the academic program ensures students build applied skills alongside their regular studies.
Boosting Employability & Career Readiness – Learners will be trained in globally relevant, industry-endorsed skills that are highly sought after by employers.
Strengthening the Local Talent Ecosystem – By nurturing 3,000+ students annually with advanced digital competencies, the initiative will contribute to creating a strong pool of talent from Tier-3 regions.
Pathway to Emerging Careers – The program will open doors to career opportunities in AI, Cloud, Cybersecurity, Data Science, and more, aligning with both national and global workforce needs.
This collaboration reflects a shared vision to democratize access to digital skills and ensure that students from Tier-3 cities are not left behind in the technology-driven future of work. It’s a step toward building a more inclusive, skilled, and employable India .
