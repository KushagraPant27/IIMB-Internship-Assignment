Highlight of the week from Nasscom
Spotlight
AI Startup Mixer | Mumbai Edition
What an inspiring evening for Mumbai’s AI ecosystem! The AI Startup Mixer hosted on December 4th, brought together some of the brightest minds shaping India’s AI future. The event, co-hosted by Nasscom AI and Google Cloud , in partnership with Society for Innovation & Entrepreneurship (SINE), IIT Bombay, was a vibrant blend of ideas, innovation, and opportunity.
With nearly 55 leaders across AI startups, venture capital, product, and research , the mixer created a dynamic space where knowledge met opportunity and collaboration sparked new possibilities.
Event Highlights
Panel on Agentic AI
Vishal Aman (Ankur Capital), Anush Prem (Inflexor Ventures), and Sudeep Kumar Das (Nasscom AI) explored the rise of agentic AI architectures—from real-world deployability to scalable startup applications.
Startup Showcase
Breakthrough innovations took center stage as SubVerse AI and Choira presented solutions creating real-world impact.
Networking That Mattered
High tea met high energy as founders, VCs, and ecosystem builders connected meaningfully. New collaborations were initiated, introductions flowed effortlessly, and ideas found new co-builders.
Vision for Next-Gen AI
Google Cloud shared practical pathways for scaling AI with efficiency, infra readiness, and faster GTM momentum. Prasad Shetty (SINE, IIT Bombay) contributed powerful insights on nurturing innovation from campus to market.
