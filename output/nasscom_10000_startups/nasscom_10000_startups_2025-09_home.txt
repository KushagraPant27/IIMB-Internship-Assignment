Nasscom InnoTrek - Empowering India’s Deep Tech Startups for Global Success
Nasscom InnoTrek serves as a strategic platform designed to unlock global market potential, build high-value connections, and enhance international visibility for India’s most promising startups. By providing access to critical knowledge, resources, and networks, InnoTrek enables emerging companies to navigate and thrive in high-growth markets.
InnoTrek 2025 – USA , presented by Nasscom DeepTech , represents the next phase in this transformative journey. The initiative will spotlight Indian startups that are developing cutting-edge Deep Tech solutions, demonstrating their ability to address complex global challenges and create meaningful industry impact.
Through curated engagements with international technology leaders, industry experts, and investors, participating startups will gain:
Exposure to global market opportunities
Insights into scaling Deep Tech solutions internationally
Access to strategic collaborations and funding networks
The #InnoTrekUS2025 cohort embodies innovation and ambition, pushing boundaries across multiple sectors and showcasing how India’s Deep Tech ecosystem is contributing to technological advancement on a global scale.
Follow their journey to witness the future of technology as these ventures engage with global stakeholders, take on new challenges, and amplify India’s presence on the world stage.
