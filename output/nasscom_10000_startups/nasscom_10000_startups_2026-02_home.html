    Democratizing AI for Inclusive Growth: India’s Roadmap to AI Leadership

    Democratizing AI is no longer aspirational, it is essential for inclusive and sustainable growth.

    At a fireside chat during the AI Impact Summit pre-event, S. Krishnan, Secretary, Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology, outlined India’s strategic roadmap for advancing its AI ecosystem and infrastructure.

    He highlighted four critical pillars shaping India’s AI journey:

    • A balanced infrastructure strategy: Leveraging global compute capabilities while simultaneously building robust domestic AI infrastructure to strengthen strategic autonomy.

    • Democratized access to AI: Ensuring compute, tools, and capabilities are accessible, affordable, and inclusive—enabling innovation beyond a few large players.

    • High-impact sector adoption: Unlocking AI-driven productivity across manufacturing, healthcare, agriculture, education, and governance—where its real-world impact is most transformative.

    • Human capital as a core advantage: Positioning India’s talent pool as its most enduring strength across the entire AI value chain.

    He emphasized that India’s success in AI will be defined not just by technological advancement, but by how effectively technology empowers productivity, innovation, and economic transformation. Through deep collaboration between government, industry, and academia, India has the opportunity to emerge as the use-case capital of the world.
