About SINE
Society for Innovation and Entrepreneurship (SINE) @ IIT Bombay offers 'Start to Scale' support. We help transform research and ideas into successful ventures.

Looking To Get Incubated?
SINE offers up to three years of incubation support for startups across diverse technologies and domains, with options for both physical and virtual incubation.

Pillars Of Support
At SINE, we power the innovators and job creators of tomorrow by offering essential pillars of support.
State of the Art Infrastructure
We provide top tier plug-and-play infrastructure with fully equipped office spaces, coworking seats, and dynamic shared spaces like meeting rooms, conference rooms, and event areas. Our ergonomic setups and advanced lab facilities are crafted to foster a collaborative and innovation-driven environment.
Explore More
Expert Mentorship
We have an extensive network of seasoned mentors across industries, including Mentors-in-Residence, ready to support our startups at every stage of their entrepreneurial journey. Our mentors provide guidance that is tailor-made for founders and startups.
Explore More
Ecosystem Connect
We Connect startups with a strong ecosystem of industry leaders, corporate and CSR partners,relevant local and international connections, service providers, manufacturing links etc., because growth of a startup thrives on the strength of a supportive ecosystem!
Explore More
Funding Opportunity
We help our startups, access a comprehensive range of funding options, including government grants, seed funding, connect to angel investors and venture capitalists, and banks. This ensures that our startups get the right financial resources to fuel their growth and scale their innovations, at the right time.
Explore More
Networking and Visibility
We create valuable networking opportunities with industry leaders and enhance visibility through events and media to boost our startup’s growth potential. Value additions include bootcamps, coaching, workshops, and targeted training sessions.

Call For Application
SINE-IDFC First Ignite Program (CSR Grant)
The Society for Innovation and Entrepreneurship (SINE) has partnered with IDFC First Bank to support early-stage startups. The IDFC FIRST Ignite - powered by SINE is a growth-stage accelerator for startups at Technology Readiness Level (TRL) 6 and above. Focused on Greentech (Sustainability), HealthTech & AI for social Impact
Call For Application
Nitto Denko Startup Innovation Challenge
The Nitto Denko Startup Innovation Challenge aims to support innovative startups aligned with Nitto Denko’s focus in “flexible sensing”, supporting them through mentorship and Proof-of-Concept (PoC) development, and enable collaborative innovation that could potentially lead to long-term strategic partnerships.
Call For Application
Biotechnology Ignition Grant Scheme
Biotechnology Ignition Grant (BIG) Scheme is flagship programme of Biotechnology Industrial Research and Assistance Council (BIRAC), Department of Biotechnology (DBT). BIG is the largest early stage biotech funding programme in India which supports early stage start-ups, researchers and entrepreneurs. The BIG supports Biotech projects from ideation to proof of concept stage.
