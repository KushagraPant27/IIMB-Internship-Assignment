Society for Innovation and Entrepreneurship (SINE) at IIT Bombay offers ‘Start to scale’ support, transforming research and ideas into successful ventures.
Overview
SINE is a Broad Spectrum Technology Business Incubator that has supported several early-stage startups across various technology domains. Backed by key government departments, including DST, Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY), Technology Development Board (TDB), Biotechnology Industry Research Assistance Council (BIRAC) - Department of Biotechnology (DBT), Ministry of Defence (MoD), and Ministry of Power (MoP),through their numerous schemes for startups. Additionally, SINE collaborates with international organizations and partners with Corporates for their Open Innovation Needs and CSR initiatives, further strengthening the ecosystem for startups.
Mission
Create an ecosystem that will foster and support innovation and knowledge-based entrepreneurship within the IIT Bombay community, leading to the creation of wealth and social value through successful ventures.
Vision
Provide an environment to translate knowledge and innovation into the creation of successful entrepreneurs.

'Star Contributor' award by the Institution's Innovation Council (IIC)
SINE IIT Bombay has been honored with the esteemed 'Star Contributor' award by the Institution's Innovation Council (IIC) at IIT Bombay in 2024, acknowledging its exceptional contributions to IIC 6.0.
AssisTech Foundation (ATF)
SINE, IIT Bombay, has received the 'Best Assistive Technology Investor of the Year' award at the AssisTech Foundation Awards 2023, recognizing innovative excellence in assistive technology.
Department of Science and Technology, Government of India.
SINE, IIT Bombay, was honored by DST for its outstanding contributions to innovation and entrepreneurship at the DST Startup Utsav event, part of the Azadi ka Amrit Mahotsav celebration in 2022.
IESA
India Electronics and Semiconductor Association honored SINE, IIT Bombay, with the 'Incubator of the Year' award at the 2019 Technovation Awards.
DST in 2009
SINE received the prestigious 'Best Incubator Award' from the DST in 2009, a testament to its excellence and a milestone that cemented its position as a leading incubator in the country.
