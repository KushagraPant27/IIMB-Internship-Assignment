13+ years of hand-holding tech startups
CIE at IIIT Hyderabad is a technology incubator with over 13+ years of rich experience in seeding, nurturing and growing deep-tech startups in emerging segments like med tech, tech4social, visual informatics, data engineering, machine learning, language technologies, gaming and robotics. CIE IIITH, with a research led innovation as its primary mandate, focuses on amplifying technology and IP by creating research collaboration and co-creation models for startups to work with research groups at IIITH. . CIE has been an ecosystem enabler fostering communities that create and scale emerging technology solutions, innovations and startups.
