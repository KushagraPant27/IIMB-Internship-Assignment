Avishkar
An acceleration program designed specifically for startups working with AI, AR / VR, Computer Vision, Machine Learning, Robotics, NLP and other deep tech.
Ojas Medtech
An Acceleration programme for startups in MedTech, Life Sciences, Bio-Technology, Diagnostics, Wellness or building devices for Health Care along with emerging technologies.
Mediatech
The accelerator programs are designed to discover, nurture and foster start-ups developing innovative solutions and AI applications in areas of Virtual Production, Metaverse, Multimedia and Journalism.
Seeding
We accelerate the growth of startups through various seeding programs like IIIT-H EIR, IIIT-H NIDHI PRAYAS, OJAS PEP and EIR (MedTech).
Women
Accelerator programs for women-led startups that provide them with a platform to leverage various opportunities, network and build a strong foundation for growth.
