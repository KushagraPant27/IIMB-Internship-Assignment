About IIMCIP
The IIM Calcutta Innovation Park (IIMCIP) is a not for profit (Section 8) company established under the aegis of IIM Calcutta to promote Entrepreneurship and Innovation.
Our Purpose
“The broad objective of IIMCIP is to actively foster innovation and socially impactful entrepreneurs and enable sustainable development”
