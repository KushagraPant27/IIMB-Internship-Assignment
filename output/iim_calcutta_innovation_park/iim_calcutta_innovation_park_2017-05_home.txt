About Us
The IIM Calcutta Innovation Park (IIMCIP) is a not for profit (Section 8) company established under the aegis of IIM Calcutta to promote Entrepreneurship and Innovation. The broad objectives of IIMCIP are to promote entrepreneurship and create a nurturing ecosystem where fledgling enterprises with innovative ideas and solutions can flourish; and To promote Innovation in research and business through industry-academia partnership.

Pacchunga University College hosts Thinking Social Seminar, March'17
IIM Calcutta Innovation Park in association with TATA group hosted Thinking Social Seminar at Pacchunga University College ,on 31st March’2017, under the aegis of TATA Social Enterprise Challenge. The primary objective of this event was to build awareness on social entrepreneurship, generate interest and inspire the young generation to come up with innovative solutions to social challenges.
