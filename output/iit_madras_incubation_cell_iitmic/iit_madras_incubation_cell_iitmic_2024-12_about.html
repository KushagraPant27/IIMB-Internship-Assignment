Incubation Cell

The IITM Incubation Cell (IITMIC) is India’s leading Deep-tech Startup hub. It coordinates and leverages the synergies of various strands of excellence driving innovation and entrepreneurship at the Institute, which consists of cutting-edge research and industrial interactions. IITMIC supports students, faculty, staff, alumni members and external entrepreneurs in creating successful deep tech startups, disrupting industries and translating benefits to the society at large.

The Incubation Cell nurtures technology ventures through their start-up phase by providing all the support necessary to help entrepreneurs establish themselves before they scale up their ventures. IITMIC supports entrepreneurs translating innovations into products and services that are commercially viable.
