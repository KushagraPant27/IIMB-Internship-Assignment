Accelerator & Funding Programs
Explore the Funding landscape with FITT and tap into some of the models most relevant for your startup to grow
Technology Incubation and Development of Entrepreneurs (TIDE 2.0)
Technology Innovation & Development of Entrepreneurship Supports (TIDES) 2.0 of the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MEITY) promotes tech entrepreneurship through financial and technical support to incubators engaged in supporting ICT startups primarily engaged in using emerging technologies.
Pfizer INDovation Program
Pfizer is committed to nurture and support ideas that can prepare the healthcare systems to be future ready. The Pfizer INDovation Program will help accelerate the journey of six start-ups into the market through customized support and by fostering relevant cross-industry collaborations.
iDEX-Innovations for Defence Excellence
An ecosystem to foster innovation & technology development in Defence and Aerospace by engaging innovators & entrepreneurs to deliver technologically advanced solutions for modernizing Indian Military.
SONA-COMSTAR-IIT DELHI INNOVATION PROGRAM (SCIDIP)
Smart, safe and clean mobility solutions are of paramount importance specially in the urban regions which are grappling with challenges.
SAMSUNG – SOLVE FOR TOMORROW PROGRAM
Samsung Solve for Tomorrow Program is a global STEM (Science, Technology, Engineering and Math) and Design Thinking based competition created to foster problem-solving skills.

Stakeholders
We handhold startups by offering Technological Support, Mentoring Support, Network Support, and Financial Support
Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY)
The Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) is an executive agency of the union government of the Republic of India.
Department of Science and Technology (DST)
The Department of Science and Technology (DST) was founded in May 1971 with the goal of promoting new areas of science and technology, with a focus on emerging fields like research and development.
Biotechnology Industry Research Assistance Council (BIRAC)
The Department of Biotechnology (DBT), Government of India, established BIRAC as an industry-academia interface. BIRAC carries out its mandate through a wide range of impact initiatives, including providing access to risk capital through targeted funding, technology transfer, IP management, and handholding programs that help biotech firms achieve innovation excellence and become globally competitive.
