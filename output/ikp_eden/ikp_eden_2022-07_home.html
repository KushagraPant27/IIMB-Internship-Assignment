Welcome to IKP EDEN

 

IKP  EDEN is a Hardware Product Startup Incubator with an in-house DIY Makerspace.

EDEN stands for Engineering, Design, and Entrepreneurship Network. We want people to learn, we want hobbyists and makers to turn into entrepreneurs and start a hardware product revolution. India has the talent; all we need is the right support network and a super makerspace to help with the prototyping. IKP – EDEN is also as a co-working space where you can meet some of the smartest minds, that will help you realize the potential of your idea.

IKP EDEN is located opposite the Forum Mall in Koramangala, Bangalore, and has hosted over 200 teams (as of March 2022) since it’s launch in 2015. 

IKP EDEN is promoted by IKP Knowledge Park. IKP EDEN is supported by Department of Science and Technology, Govt. of India, under the NIDHI-TBI Scheme and the NIDHI-PRAYAS Scheme. IKP EDEN is also supported by BIRAC, Dept of Biotechnology, Govt. of India under the BioNEST program, the BIRAC Seed Fund Program, and the NIDHI Seed Support Program.

With support from the Dept of IT, BT, Govt of Karnataka, IKP Knowledge Park has setup K-Tech Innovation hubs along the lines of EDEN  in Rajaji Nagar (BLR), Mysore, Belgaum, Mangalore,  and Shimoga.

Come join the community of over 350 startups across Karnataka.
