How we help Incubators (From Collaborate with us)
University Pitch Sessions
Every week, we invite university startups to pitch in front of our network. Last year, 1,300+ university startups pitched to an audience of 1,000+ organizations and 2,500+ venture capitalists in these sessions.
Culture of Innovation
By being associated with Plug and Play’s brand through our accelerators on campus, we inspire the next generation of entrepreneurs. We strive to show students there are no boundaries to their future success.
Career Growth
We recruit interns and host career fairs to connect students with opportunities within our global network. This opens job opportunities for students and provides them with valuable skills and knowledge.
Industry Connections
Through networking, meetups, and other events with corporations and governments, we provide new perspectives and serve as a valuable resource for students and researchers eager to advance their ideas.
Impactful Research
Collaborating with industry partners allows universities to conduct research directly relevant to real-world challenges. This research generates knowledge that can lead to new technologies and products.

How we help startups
Investment Opportunities
With us you can explore funding options that fit your stage and vision. We help you access opportunities to raise cpital through curated investor connects and pitch opportunities. This support can fast-track your startup’s journey to the next level.
Accelerator Programs
Join structured programs designed to support early and growth-stage startups. Gain access to expert mentorship, tools, and industry connections to scale your business. Our programs help you move faster and smarter.
Business Development
Collaborate with industry leaders and large organizations for real market exposure. We help you make the best of partnerships that open up growth and pilot opportunities for startups. This is where innovation meet industry and turn into business outcomes.
Challenge Offerings
Tap into opportunities to apply to and leverage sector specific challenges to showcase your solutions. These platforms help you gain visibility, pilot opportunities, and connect with clients. It’s a great way to prove your value while solving real-world problems.
