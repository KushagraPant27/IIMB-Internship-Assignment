Our Story
Founded in 1973, IIM Bangalore is known to be one of India’s best business schools. Facilitating a 100 acre campus in the Garden City of India, the institute creates a playground for the nation’s brightest innovators and problem solvers.
NSRCEL is IIM Bangalore’s flagship business incubator that is a non-profit institution purely focused on aiding creators and funding startups in their journey. With nearly 2813 ventures incubated that exceed $7.37 Billion in combined value, there’s nothing stopping you from embarking on your entrepreneurial path.
We’re building a space conducive to entrepreneurial collaboration and growth, well-reflected in our recently awarded Great Place To Work® Certification . We build bigger and do it better. NSRCEL shows you that the impossible is just a benchmark!
