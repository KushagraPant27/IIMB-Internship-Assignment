At NSRCEL, we believe founders are the driving force behind India’s tech future. But scaling something extraordinary isn’t easy. The startup journey is as demanding as it is rewarding—and that’s where we come in. For 20 years, we’ve been more than just an incubator—we’re a launchpad for tech innovation, a network of industry leaders, and a powerhouse of resources designed to help entrepreneurs survive, scale, and thrive. Because when startups succeed, India leads the way.
